Here are some of the specification if F-35.

  1. Aircraft Type: 5th-generation single-seat, single-engine, all-weather stealth multirole combat aircraft designed for air superiority and strike missions.
  2. Primary Contractor: Lockheed Martin, operating alongside principal aerospace partners Northrop Grumman and BAE Systems.
  3. F-35A Variant.: Conventional Takeoff and Landing (CTOL) model built for standard runways, primarily operated by air forces, and rated for up to 9.0 Gs.
  4. F-35B Variant.: Short Takeoff and Vertical Landing (STOVL) model featuring a massive lift-fan system for amphibious assault ships and short runways.
  5. F-35C Variant.: Carrier Variant (CV) featuring larger folding wings, reinforced landing gear, and an arrestor hook for catapult launches and carrier recoveries.
  6. Top Speed.: Mach 1.6 (~1,200 mph / 1,930 km/h) at altitude.
  7. Propulsion System.: Single Pratt & Whitney F135 turbofan engine delivering up to 43,000 lbs of thrust.
  8. Payload Capacity.: Holds over 18,000 lbs of weapons across a combination of internal bays (for stealth) and external wing pylons.
  9. Low-Observable Stealth.: Employs alignment-based radar-busting geometry and advanced radar-absorbent coatings to minimize radar cross-section.
  10. Advanced Sensor Fusion.: Automatically merges tracking data from the AESA radar, targeting systems, and 360-degree cameras into a single tactical picture.
  11. Helmet Mounted Display.: Projects critical flight parameters and sensor imagery directly onto the pilot's visor, allowing them to look completely through the cockpit floor.
  12. Global Fleet Scale.: Over 1,340 aircraft delivered worldwide, surpassing 1 million cumulative flight hours across roughly 20 international partner nations.
